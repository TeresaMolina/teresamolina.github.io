About Me

Welcome to my portfolio! Take a look around and explore my work in visual design, content creation, and digital branding.

Self-Taught Visual Artist

I’m a self-taught artist with years of experience bringing characters and concepts to life. My past artwork includes fan pieces for major creators like Markiplier and Jacksepticeye (which were even recognized by the creators themselves!). I’ve also built a universe of original characters over the years—including Absalom, who stars in my custom animated stream screens.

Streaming & Entrepreneurship

Between 2020 and 2023, I built a dedicated community streaming Dead by Daylight on Twitch. That hands-on experience in content creation led me to launch Sakura Starline Studios, a digital art studio focused on providing affordable, custom graphics and channel branding for rising streamers. You could say I’m a true Digital Media Specialist, combining creativity, technical skill, and community building.
